How we work

A Florida company buying for itself

We buy with our own money, for our own portfolio. Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Are you real estate agents?

No. We are not licensed brokers or sales associates, we do not list property, we represent nobody and we charge no commission. We buy for our own account.

Can I invest with you?

No. We use our own capital and accept no investor funds. There is no offering here, and nothing on this site is a solicitation.

Will you buy a house that needs work?

That is mostly what we buy. We would rather see the real condition than a staged one, and unpermitted work needs discussing rather than hiding.

How fast can you close?

It depends on title and inspection, and we will give you an honest timeline rather than a number designed to win the deal.

¿Hablan español?

Sí. Negotiations, documents and tenancy matters are handled in Spanish or English, whichever you prefer.

Do you flip houses?

No. We renovate and hold as long-term rentals, which is why we spend money on roofs and systems rather than only on what photographs well.

Thinking of selling?

A direct conversation and a written offer with our reasoning. No commission, no obligation.
